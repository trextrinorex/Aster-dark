# AFTER DARK

An immersive, data-driven 3D experience exploring Earth's disappearing night and global light pollution.

## Vision

AFTER DARK treats Earth as the protagonist: a cinematic journey from natural darkness to an illuminated planet, followed by exploration of the science, history, impacts, and ways to restore the night.

## Status

🚧 Active build — initial foundation.

## Planned experience

- Cinematic 3D Earth and atmosphere
- Global night-light visualization
- Historical timeline
- Country and city exploration
- Interactive night-sky simulation
- Light-pollution science and ecology
- Restore-the-night scenario
- Responsive and accessible experience
